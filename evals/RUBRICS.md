# Arab Writer v1.1 Evaluation Rubrics

Use a **core rubric + task-specific rubric**. Do not collapse everything into one fluency score.

## Core (all cases)
Score 0–2 each:
1. Fidelity to source facts and intent
2. Instruction compliance
3. Arabic correctness

For high-fidelity cases, a material protected-fact or protected-relation error is a case failure regardless of total score.

## Task-specific dimensions

### Proofread
- minimality of edits
- error correction coverage

### Naturalize
- reduction of formulaic/stiff prose
- preservation of meaning and voice

### Academic
- epistemic calibration
- citation/terminology integrity
- scholarly cohesion

### Financial / policy / legal
- anchored value preservation
- modality/condition preservation
- decision-useful clarity

### Executive
- decision clarity
- materiality
- actionability
- brevity

### Voice Lock
- stylistic similarity to authentic samples
- absence of generic-polish drift

### Saudi/dialect-sensitive
- pragmatic fit
- regional naturalness without caricature
- correct formality/hierarchy

## Evaluation method
Prefer hybrid evaluation:
- deterministic fidelity checks;
- blinded human preference/scoring;
- optional LLM judge as an additional signal, not the sole authority.
