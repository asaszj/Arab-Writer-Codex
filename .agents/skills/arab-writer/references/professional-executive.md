# Professional and Executive Arabic

## Professional correspondence

Make these visible when present:
- purpose;
- requested action;
- attachment/reference;
- deadline;
- decision needed;
- next step.

Courtesy should not bury the action.

Prefer:
`أرفق لسعادتكم الخطاب للاطلاع، ونأمل الرفع بطلب الموافقة على المشاركة.`

Over:
long ceremonial introductions that delay the request.

## Reports

Distinguish:
- fact;
- issue;
- analysis;
- implication;
- recommendation;
- owner;
- deadline.

Do not mix a recommendation into a factual paragraph without signaling it.

## Executive writing

Lead with decision-relevant information.

A useful order:
1. decision / issue;
2. impact;
3. evidence;
4. options;
5. recommendation;
6. next action.

Use tables or bullets when comparing options, owners, deadlines, risks, or metrics. Use prose for reasoning.

## Board material

Keep:
- financial basis explicit;
- assumptions labeled;
- unresolved dependencies visible;
- recommendation separate from management fact;
- dates and governance approvals exact.

Avoid:
- vague "تمت مناقشة عدة أمور";
- unquantified "تكلفة بسيطة" when values are available;
- conclusions unsupported by the presented evidence.
