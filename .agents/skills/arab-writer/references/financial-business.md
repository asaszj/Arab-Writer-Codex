# Financial and Business Arabic

## Preserve numerical meaning

Protect:
- currency;
- amount;
- percentage;
- period;
- unit;
- gross/net basis;
- VAT status;
- actual/forecast/estimate;
- monthly/annual basis;
- decimal precision.

Never "tidy" a number by changing its value.

## Financial statements and analysis

Keep terminology consistent:
- revenue / الإيرادات;
- gross profit / مجمل الربح;
- operating profit / الربح التشغيلي;
- net profit / صافي الربح;
- cash flow / التدفق النقدي;
- receivable / ذمم مدينة or مدينون according to context;
- payable / ذمم دائنة or دائنون according to context.

Do not mix cash and accrual concepts.

## Forecasts

Mark assumptions as assumptions.
Preserve scenario labels.
Do not convert forecast language into guaranteed outcomes.

## Business cases

Separate:
- market evidence;
- internal assumption;
- modeled estimate;
- recommendation.

A credible business paragraph states the basis of the number, not only the number.

## Nonprofit finance

Distinguish:
- donation;
- grant;
- sponsorship;
- fee for service;
- beneficiary contribution;
- in-kind support;
- earned revenue.

Do not blur these categories because governance and accounting treatment may differ.
