# Missed-Opportunity Review

For `rewrite`, `naturalize`, `voice-lock` with editorial editing, and `document` mode, run a final adversarial review after the candidate is already safe.

The reviewer does **not** rewrite immediately. It searches for remaining material issues:
- awkward but grammatical phrasing;
- near-semantic repetition;
- overloaded factual sentences;
- weak or incomplete transitions;
- ambiguous pronouns;
- meta-sentences that describe content after it has already been presented;
- bibliography inconsistency;
- table/prose duplication;
- unnecessary chronological repetition.

Then edit only findings that are clearly beneficial and safe. Preserve intentional pedagogical repetition.

Principle: **Safe does not mean finished; more change does not mean better.**
