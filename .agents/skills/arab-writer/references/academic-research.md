# Academic and Research Arabic

## Evidence integrity

Never invent:
- citations;
- references;
- DOI values;
- quotations;
- sample sizes;
- coefficients;
- p-values;
- methods;
- dates;
- institutional names;
- findings.

If support is missing, improve the sentence linguistically without manufacturing evidence.

## Calibrate claim strength

Preserve the distinction between:
- description: `يعرض`, `يوضح`;
- association: `يرتبط`, `يتزامن`;
- statistical association: `تشير النتائج إلى علاقة`;
- possibility: `قد`, `يمكن أن`;
- inference: `يرجح`, `تشير النتائج`;
- causation: `يؤدي`, `يسبب`;
- proof: `يثبت`.

Do not upgrade association to causation.

## Literature review

Prefer synthesis by:
- theme;
- theory;
- context;
- method;
- convergence/divergence;
- research gap.

Avoid a sequence of disconnected study summaries when the evidence supports synthesis.

## Methods and results

Preserve:
- construct names;
- hypotheses;
- variable labels;
- model names;
- equations;
- tables;
- statistical notation.

Do not turn non-significant results into meaningful effects.

## Discussion

Separate:
- observed finding;
- interpretation;
- comparison with literature;
- implication;
- limitation.

Do not imply a mechanism that the design did not test.

## Reviewer responses

For each comment:
1. acknowledge the point without unnecessary flattery;
2. state the exact change;
3. identify location;
4. explain rationale if needed.

Do not claim a revision was made unless it was made.
