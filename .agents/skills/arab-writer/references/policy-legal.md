# Policy, Legal, and Regulatory Arabic

This reference improves wording; it does not create legal advice.

## High-fidelity rules

Preserve exactly:
- defined terms;
- article and clause numbers;
- regulator names;
- license numbers;
- statutory dates;
- mandatory versus optional wording;
- exceptions;
- conditions;
- thresholds;
- notice periods.

## Modal verbs matter

Do not interchange:
- `يجب`;
- `يلزم`;
- `يجوز`;
- `يمكن`;
- `ينبغي`;
- `يُحظر`;
- `يحق`;
- `قد`.

Changing these can change legal effect.

## Policy writing

Prefer clauses that identify:
- actor;
- obligation;
- trigger;
- deadline;
- evidence/record;
- exception;
- escalation.

Avoid vague subjects such as `يتم العمل على` when responsibility matters.

## Regulatory claims

Do not infer that a common practice is a legal requirement.
If the source says `وفقًا للممارسة`, do not rewrite it as `وفقًا للائحة`.
Preserve citation to the regulatory source when provided.
