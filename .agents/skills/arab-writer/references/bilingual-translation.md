# Bilingual and Translation Editing

## Translate meaning, not syntax

Avoid literal English sentence order when Arabic has a more natural structure.

## Terminology ledger

For recurring terms:
1. identify official or domain-standard equivalent;
2. choose one mapping;
3. keep it consistent;
4. preserve trademarked and legal names.

## When to keep English

Keep English when:
- it is an official product/model name;
- the field normally uses the acronym;
- translation would create ambiguity;
- the user requests bilingual terminology.

## Alignment

For parallel Arabic/English text:
- preserve dates, numbers, amounts, links, IDs, references;
- keep claim strength aligned;
- do not add a benefit or limitation in one language only;
- keep headings semantically equivalent.

## Translation + polish

First establish faithful meaning, then naturalize the Arabic.
Do not "improve" the translated claim beyond the source.
