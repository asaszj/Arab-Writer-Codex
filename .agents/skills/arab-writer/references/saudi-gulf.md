# Saudi and Gulf Context

Use this reference only when the audience or context is Saudi/Gulf.

## Institutional register

Use clear Modern Standard Arabic suitable for:
- government;
- nonprofit;
- corporate;
- academic;
- professional correspondence.

Respect official Saudi entity and program names exactly as provided.

## Courtesy

Use role-appropriate courtesy without excessive ceremonial padding.
`سعادة` and formal salutations may be appropriate in official correspondence, but do not multiply honorifics unless protocol requires it.

## Dates and identifiers

Preserve:
- Hijri/Gregorian designation;
- national IDs;
- license numbers;
- CR numbers;
- VAT numbers;
- official initiative names.

Do not convert calendars unless asked.

## Business Arabic

Prefer direct professional wording common in Saudi organizations:
- `نأمل التوجيه`;
- `نأمل الرفع`;
- `أرفق لسعادتكم`;
- `للاطلاع واتخاذ ما يلزم`;
only when they match the actual hierarchy and task.

## Regional neutrality

Do not insert Saudi dialect into formal MSA.
Do not force Saudi phrasing on content aimed at the wider Arab world.
