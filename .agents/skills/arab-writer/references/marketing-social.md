# Marketing and Social Writing

## Objective first

Identify the actual action:
- awareness;
- click;
- register;
- attend;
- purchase;
- donate;
- inquire;
- follow;
- share.

Do not add a CTA the user did not want.

## Natural promotional Arabic

Prefer:
- specific benefit;
- concrete proof;
- clear audience;
- one primary action.

Avoid:
- inflated superlatives without evidence;
- generic "رحلة استثنائية";
- repeated emoji;
- hashtag stuffing;
- unsupported scarcity or urgency.

## Social posts

Match the platform and audience.
Preserve names, handles, event dates, sponsor names, and official titles.
Do not make a public claim stronger than the source.

## Brand voice

When a source sample exists, infer:
- formality;
- vocabulary;
- sentence length;
- emoji use;
- Arabic/English mixing;
- CTA style.

Keep the pattern consistent across the output.
